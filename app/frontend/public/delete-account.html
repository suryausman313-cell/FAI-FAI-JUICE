Fai Fai Juice - Account Deletion Request

This page lets a Fai Fai Juice customer request deletion of their app account and associated personal data.

What will be deleted

After we verify the request, we will delete the customer account and personal data that we are not legally required to retain. Some transaction, accounting, fraud-prevention, tax or legal records may be retained when required.

What happens next

A WhatsApp message will open with your request. Send it to Fai Fai Juice. We may verify that you own the account before deletion. Requests will normally be handled within 30 days, subject to lawful retention requirements.
