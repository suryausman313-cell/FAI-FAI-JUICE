Fai Fai Juice Privacy Policy Last updated: 21 August 2026 This Privacy Policy applies to the Fai Fai Juice customer ordering app and website, operated by Fai Fai Juice in Fujairah, United Arab Emirates. It explains what information we collect, why we use it, how it may be shared, how long it may be kept, and the choices available to users. 1. Information we collect Depending on the features you use, we may collect or process: Customer name and mobile phone number. Account information used to sign in, including a customer PIN. The service is designed to store authentication credentials securely rather than exposing the PIN as plain text. Order information, including ordered items, quantities, prices, notes, order status, pickup or delivery choice, and order history. Delivery information, including delivery address and a location selected or shared by the customer when delivery is requested. Feedback or reviews submitted through the app. Notification subscription information when a customer chooses to enable order notifications. Technical and security logs that may be processed as needed to operate, protect, troubleshoot, and improve the service. 2. Location information Location is used only when relevant to delivery functionality. If you choose delivery and allow location access, the app may use your selected or device-provided location to determine delivery availability, calculate delivery information, and help the rider fulfil the order. You can deny location permission, but some delivery features may then require manual location selection or may not work fully. 3. Notifications If you enable notifications, notification subscription information may be stored so that we can send order-status updates such as accepted, preparing, ready, pickup, rider, or delivery updates. You can disable notifications in your browser or device settings. 4. How we use information We use information to: Create and manage customer accounts. Process pickup and delivery orders. Provide order tracking and customer support. Calculate delivery availability and charges. Send requested order notifications. Prevent misuse, protect accounts, troubleshoot problems, and maintain service reliability. Meet accounting, legal, tax, or regulatory obligations where applicable. 5. Payments If online card or wallet payments are offered, payment processing may be provided by an external payment provider. Payment-card details may be entered directly into the payment provider's service. Fai Fai Juice does not need to store a customer's full payment-card number when payment is processed by that provider. Payment status and transaction references may be retained with the order where needed. 6. Sharing of information We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with people or service providers involved in operating the service, such as Fai Fai Juice staff, kitchen personnel, delivery riders, hosting/database providers, notification services, and payment providers when online payments are used. Information may also be disclosed when required by law. 7. Data security We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect customer information, including access controls and secure authentication practices. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. 8. Retention and deletion Personal data is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above. Some order, payment, accounting, fraud-prevention, or legal records may need to be retained for a lawful period even after an account deletion request. Data that is not required to be retained will be deleted or anonymised as appropriate. Customers may request deletion of their Fai Fai Juice account and associated personal data using our Account Deletion Request page. We may verify account ownership before processing the request. 9. Children's privacy The app is a general food and beverage ordering service and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from young children without appropriate consent. 10. Changes to this policy We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date. 11.
