Fai Fai Juice Privacy Policy Last updated: 21 August 2026 Fai Fai Juice respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Fai Fai Juice ordering app and how that information is used. Information we may collect When you place an order or use app features, we may collect information you provide such as your name, phone number, delivery address, delivery location, order details, notes, and feedback. Location information If you choose delivery and allow location access, the app may use your device location to help you select a delivery location. Location access is used only for delivery-related functionality. Notifications If you enable notifications, the app may store notification subscription information so we can send updates about your order, such as accepted, preparing, ready, or delivery status. How we use information We use the information to process and manage orders, provide delivery and pickup services, communicate order updates, improve the app, respond to customer requests, prevent misuse, and maintain service reliability. Payments If online payments are enabled, payment processing may be handled by a third-party payment provider. Fai Fai Juice does not need to store your full card number when payments are processed by an external payment provider. Sharing of information We may share only the information necessary to fulfil an order with relevant staff, kitchen personnel, delivery riders, service providers, or payment providers. We do not sell personal information. Data retention Order and account information may be retained for business, customer-service, security, accounting, and legal purposes for as long as reasonably necessary. Your choices You can deny location or notification permissions in your device settings. Some app features may not work fully if the required permission is disabled. Security We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information handled through the app. Children The app is intended for general customers ordering food and beverages and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from young children without appropriate consent. Changes to this policy We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page. Contact If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact Fai Fai Juice using the contact details available in the Fai Fai Juice app.
