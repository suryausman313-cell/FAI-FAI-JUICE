Fai Fai Juice Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Fai Fai Juice customer ordering app and website, operated by Fai Fai Juice in Fujairah, United Arab Emirates.

1. Information we collect

  • Customer name and mobile phone number.
  • Account information used to sign in, including a customer PIN. Authentication credentials are stored securely rather than exposing the PIN as plain text.
  • Order details, order history, pickup or delivery choice, notes and feedback.
  • Delivery address and delivery location when delivery is requested.
  • Notification subscription information when notifications are enabled.
  • Technical and security logs needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.

2. Location information

If you choose delivery and allow location access, the app may use your selected or device-provided location to determine delivery availability and help fulfil the order. You can deny location permission, but some delivery features may not work fully.

3. Notifications

If you enable notifications, we may store notification subscription information so we can send order-status updates. You can disable notifications in your browser or device settings.

4. How we use information

  • Create and manage customer accounts.
  • Process pickup and delivery orders.
  • Provide order tracking and customer support.
  • Calculate delivery availability and charges.
  • Send requested order notifications.
  • Prevent misuse, protect accounts and maintain service reliability.
  • Meet accounting, legal, tax or regulatory obligations where applicable.

5. Payments

If online payments are offered, payment processing may be handled by an external payment provider. Fai Fai Juice does not need to store a customer's full payment-card number when payment is processed by that provider.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with Fai Fai Juice staff, kitchen personnel, delivery riders, hosting/database providers, notification services and payment providers, or when required by law.

7. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect customer information, including access controls and secure authentication practices.

8. Retention and deletion

Personal data is kept only for as long as reasonably needed. Some transaction, accounting, fraud-prevention, tax or legal records may need to be retained for a lawful period.

Customers may request deletion of their Fai Fai Juice account and associated personal data using our Account Deletion Request page.

9. Children's privacy

The app is a general food and beverage ordering service and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from young children without appropriate consent.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
