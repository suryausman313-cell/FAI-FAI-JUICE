### The Neapolitan Difference

What sets Neapolitan pizza apart? It's a combination of specific dough preparation, high-quality ingredients, and a quick bake in an extremely hot oven. This process creates a pizza with a soft, elastic crust, often with leopard-spotted charring (known as "cornicione"), and a moist, flavorful center. At Vita Napoli, we adhere strictly to these traditions, ensuring that every pizza that leaves our kitchen meets the highest standards of Neapolitan authenticity. This dedication to tradition is what makes our Italian pizza in Fujairah truly exceptional.
